Customer Segmentation Analysis

Overview:
For this project, it is essential to carry out the clustering of the customers of the online store using the K-Means clustering method. There are a total of 50,000 records in the database containing information related to demographic details and purchase of customers. The main aim of the current assignment is to cluster similar customers.

Dataset:
Even when considering all these variables based on characteristics of the customers available in the data set, I decided to do my analysis using only three behavioral variables which are Recency, Frequency, and Monetary Variables since these variables are determined by customer behavior.

The Customer Segments:
The five groups were formed from the clusters. The first group is Champions, and it is made up of 17,165 customers that are new, frequent and high valued. The second group is Recent Mid-Value, and it is made up of 12,949 customers that are recent buyers, mid frequency, and mid-valued. The third group is At-Risk High-Value, and it is made up of 9,626 customers that were high valued before, but now are at risk. There are 5,592 Recent Low-Value customers that are recent buyers, infrequent and low valued.

Insights:
The Champions constitute the majority of our customer base, which is a very good thing. The most significant category of customer to our company will be the “At-Risk High-Value” category of customers. The reason for this is that they are quite valuable in that they have high scores in terms of both frequency and monetary value, but they have not made any purchases in some time.

Recommendations:
Emphasis on incentives such as exclusivity, early access, loyalty awards, and premium plans should be made in the case of The Champions. As far as Mid-Value customers go, who are the latest additions, promotion campaigns might turn out to be helpful. As far as At-Risk High-Value customers go, win-back campaigns with special discounts and loyalty awards can prove to be quite beneficial. As far as latest low-value customers go, cross-selling, referrals, and special offers might be helpful. Finally, in case of At-Risk Mid-Value customers, re-engagement email campaigns can turn out to be useful.

Tools used:
This project has been implemented using Python, Jupyter notebook, Pandas and NumPy libraries for handling the data, Matplotlib and Seaborn libraries for visualizing the data and Scikit-learn library for K-means and StandardScaler.
