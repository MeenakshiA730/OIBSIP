Retail Sales Data Analysis

Overview:
The present study is grounded on the technique of data analysis according to the sales made by the retail shops with 1000 observations during the period from January 2023 to January 2024. The primary purpose of data analysis is to detect the trends in sales, collect customer information, and identify the most profitable product. Moreover, I would like to analyze the dependence between numeric variables.

Dataset:
TransactionID, Date, CustomerID, Gender, Age, ProductCategory, Quantity, UnitPrice, and TotalAmount make up the data set. The drawbacks of the data set include the absence of the name or code of the product, and it is impossible to organize the products. All the analysis was conducted by using product categories.

Tools used:
The analysis was conducted using Python code written in the Jupyter notebook environment with Pandas and NumPy packages manipulating the data and Matplotlib & Seaborn packages for visualization.

Insights:
    Highest sales were made in May, which means ₹53,150, while lowest sales were made in September which amounted to ₹23,620. The quarter four (Q4) is the most profitable quarter, as it produced sales worth ₹126,190.
When it comes to sales of customers according to age-wise, it was found that highest sales were made by the customers of age 45-54, which amounted to ₹97,235. Gender female had made the higher sales than gender male. However, age group 18-24 made the highest sales per transaction which was ₹501.01.
When it comes to sales of products wise, it was found that highest sales were made from the product category of electronics which amounted to ₹156,905. Through correlation analysis, price per unit has very strong positive correlation with the total amount which is 0.85.

Recommendations:
Taking into account the results received, it seems to be quite natural to move further to analyzing the reasons for the success in Q4, and in this situation, success will once again be achieved at another period. It is particularly crucial to pay special attention to the such categories as Electronics and Clothing because they show good success in the matter of sales. The use of various strategies regarding different age groups can bring the successful result due to the fact that the purchase strategy differs depending on the age of the person.
